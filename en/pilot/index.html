Week 1

Preparation

We adapt your questionnaire to WhatsApp.

  • Import of your XLSForm, Word document or paper form
  • Consent text approved with you
  • Ngambay version proposed by AI and reviewed by a speaker (in testing)
  • Full test on your phones
Week 2

Launch

Respondents receive the survey.

  • Invitations to your contact list
  • Or a keyword and QR code on a poster
  • Dashboard walkthrough with your enumerators
Week 3

Collection

Answers arrive live.

  • Answers by button, text or voice note

  • Progress check with us

Report

You leave with your data.

  • Excel export and audio files
  • Response rate, share of voice answers, measured transcription accuracy
  • Recommendations for next steps

In practice

What you bring, what you get.

You bring

Very little, and nothing technical.

  • 1
    Your questionnaireKoBoToolbox or ODK XLSForm, a Word document, or a paper form.
  • 2
    Your respondentsA list of numbers with their consent, or a place to put up a QR code.
  • 3
    A focal pointOne person we can reach during the pilot.
  • 4
    One or two enumeratorsTo listen to and check voice notes, on a computer or phone.

You get

A survey run end to end.

  • 1
    A secure workspaceA dashboard for your team only.
  • 2
    Your dataExcel or CSV and audio files; phone numbers replaced by a code.
  • 3
    A report with figuresAnswers received, share of voice answers, accuracy measured by your enumerators.
  • 4
    SupportFrom preparation to report, on WhatsApp and video calls.

Your data stays yours. Every respondent consents before the first question and can stop at any time by writing STOP. Audio is deleted at the end of the pilot if you ask, and nothing is reused for research without respondents' separate consent.

Questions

Before you start.

Is the pilot really free?

Yes, for a first pilot. If you invite a contact list, WhatsApp charges for invitation messages at Meta's rates; we tell you before launch. With a QR code or keyword, respondents write first.

Not all our respondents can write.

They can answer by tapping a button or sending a voice note. Your enumerators then check the voice notes.

Do we need to install anything?

No. Respondents use WhatsApp. Your team uses the dashboard in a browser.

What happens after the pilot?

You decide: stop, or continue with the Organisation plan, quoted on your volume.
