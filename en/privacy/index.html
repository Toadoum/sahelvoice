Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what data SahelVoice processes, why, and how you stay in control. It covers the sahelvoice.app website, the app.sahelvoice.app platform and surveys run on WhatsApp.

1. Who we are

SahelVoice is a service of Quantum Qube Technologies, a sole proprietorship registered in N'Djamena, Chad (RCCM TD-NDJ-01-2024-A10-01446). Contact: contact@sahelvoice.app.

Surveys are run on behalf of client organisations (NGOs, companies, institutions). For survey answers, the organisation running the survey decides its content and how answers are used; SahelVoice processes this data for it, on its instructions.

2. Data we process

If you answer a survey on WhatsApp:

  • your WhatsApp number and the profile name WhatsApp sends us;
  • your answers: buttons tapped, text typed, voice notes;
  • the automatic transcript of your voice notes, and any corrections made by enumerators;
  • the date and time of your answers and of your consent.

If you use the platform (staff of an organisation): your name, email address, role and a protected password (never stored in plain text).

If you visit sahelvoice.app: the website sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Forms (pilot request, feedback) prepare a message that you send yourself by WhatsApp or email: nothing is stored on the website.

3. Why we process it

  • To run the survey you agreed to answer and pass the results to the organisation running it.
  • To transcribe voice notes and let enumerators check them.
  • To respect your choice to stop: a number that wrote STOP is never contacted again.
  • Only with separate, optional consent: to reuse voice notes, without your name or number, to improve speech recognition in French and the languages of Chad. Saying no changes nothing for the survey.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

4. Consent and stopping

Consent is asked before the first question. Without it, no answer is recorded and you appear in no results. At any time, write STOP: you will receive no more messages, from any organisation. Write REPRENDRE if you change your mind.

5. Retention

  • Voice notes are deleted after the period set by the organisation (90 days by default), unless you gave the separate research consent.
  • Typed answers are kept as long as the organisation needs them for its survey, then deleted at its request.
  • Staff accounts are deleted at the organisation's request.

6. Who has access

  • Only signed-in members of the organisation running the survey can see its answers. Phone numbers are hidden in the dashboard and replaced by a code in exports.
  • Our technical providers, only to run the service:
    • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivering WhatsApp messages;
    • Amazon Web Services: hosting the platform and data, Europe (Paris) region;
    • Cloudflare: domain names and forwarding of emails sent to sahelvoice.app;
    • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): sending the platform's emails (invitations, password resets, response summaries to organisations). Survey respondents receive no email;
    • Hugging Face: French–Ngambay machine translation of questionnaire texts only. Respondents' answers are never sent there.

Voice notes are transcribed on our own servers, not by a third-party service.

7. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), dashboard access through personal accounts, hashed passwords, account lock after repeated failed attempts, two-step login, a log of sign-ins and exports, audio available only to members of the organisation concerned, pseudonymised numbers in exports, signature checks on messages received from WhatsApp, and daily encrypted backups. If an incident affects data, the organisations concerned are informed within 72 hours.

Organisations using SahelVoice accept our partner terms.

8. Your rights and data deletion

To request access to, correction or deletion of your data:

  • write STOP on WhatsApp to stop being contacted;
  • email contact@sahelvoice.app with the WhatsApp number concerned. We delete your answers, voice notes and transcripts within 30 days and inform the organisation that ran the survey.

You can also contact the organisation that invited you to answer directly.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. Client organisations are informed of significant changes.
