Partner terms

Version of 11 November 2026

These terms apply to every organisation that creates a SahelVoice workspace (NGO, agency, company, institution, university, association). By creating your workspace's admin account, you accept them on behalf of your organisation.

1. The service

SahelVoice runs surveys on WhatsApp: people answer by typing, tapping a button or sending a voice note. The service is provided by Quantum Qube Technologies (N'Djamena, RCCM TD-NDJ-01-2024-A10-01446). Every organisation uses the same SahelVoice WhatsApp number; each survey's keyword opens the right survey.

2. Who does what with the data

  • Your organisation decides the survey, the questions and how answers are used: it is the data controller.
  • SahelVoice hosts and processes answers on your behalf, only to run the survey, following your instructions. We use your data for nothing else.
  • Our technical providers are listed in the privacy policy (Meta, AWS, Cloudflare, Brevo). Voice transcription and Ngambay translation run on our own servers.

3. People's consent

  • You only invite people who agreed to be contacted by your organisation. No bought lists, no numbers collected elsewhere.
  • Each person agrees on WhatsApp before the first question. If they decline, nothing is recorded.
  • STOP ends all messages, for every organisation, permanently. You do not try to reach someone who wrote STOP by other means for the same survey.
  • You follow WhatsApp Business rules (no unsolicited advertising, no bulk messages without consent).

4. What is not allowed

  • Asking for passwords, codes, bank card or ID numbers.
  • Misleading questionnaires, propaganda, harassment, or any content that is illegal in Chad.
  • Collecting sensitive data (health, religion, origin, political opinions, victim status) without a real need, without explaining it clearly in the consent message, or without suitable protection.
  • Sharing your access, or using the platform for an organisation other than yours.

5. Your accounts

Each person has their own account. Your workspace administrator manages the team and removes access for people who leave. We strongly advise two-step login for administrators. Tell us at contact@sahelvoice.app if you think an account has been compromised.

6. Your data belongs to you

  • You export your answers at any time (CSV, Excel, audio). Phone numbers are pseudonymised in exports.
  • Voice notes are deleted automatically after the period set in your questionnaire (30 days by default).
  • You can ask for your workspace to be deleted: we send you a full export, then erase surveys, answers, audio and accounts within 30 days. Only the STOP list is kept, to protect people.
  • Answers are used to train language models only if the person gave a separate, explicit consent, and only if your questionnaire provides for it.

7. Security and incidents

We protect the platform (encrypted connections, hashed passwords, lock after repeated attempts, activity log, daily backups). If an incident affects your data, we tell you within 72 hours what we know and what we are doing.

8. Pilot, prices and WhatsApp costs

The pilot is free: one questionnaire, up to 4 weeks, support included. Beyond that, prices are set by quote. Messages your organisation sends first (invitations) may be charged by Meta; we tell you before anything is sent.

9. Machine translation

The Ngambay translation is an automatic draft, still being improved. A speaker must review and approve it before use. You remain responsible for the texts sent to people.

10. Suspension

We may suspend a workspace if these terms are not respected, after serious complaints from people contacted, or if security is at risk. Except in an emergency, we warn you first. A suspension blocks sign-ins and closes surveys; data is kept and can still be exported.

11. Liability

We do our best to keep the service running, but it also depends on WhatsApp, networks and our providers. During the pilot, the service is provided as is. Our liability cannot exceed the amounts paid for the service over the last 12 months.

12. Governing law and contact

These terms are governed by Chadian law; in case of disagreement we first seek an amicable solution, then the courts of N'Djamena have jurisdiction. Questions: contact@sahelvoice.app.
