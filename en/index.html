AI listens. Your team decides.

In Chad, many people would rather speak than write. SahelVoice transcribes their voice notes and keeps a person in the loop for whatever the machine is unsure about.

Listens

A speech recognition model transcribes each voice note within seconds, on our own servers.

« nous sommes sept à la maison »

Understands

It extracts the expected answer: a number said in words, a yes or a no, a choice, a rating.

taille_menage = 7

Checks

A spoken number is read back to the person. Anything still uncertain goes to your team's review queue.

« You said: 7. Is that right? » → Yes

Your results in Excel, Power BI or your scripts.

Each organisation creates its API keys from the dashboard.

  • Read-only. A key reads your surveys; it cannot send or change anything.
  • No phone numbers. Each person has a code.
  • One key per tool. Replaced without downtime, revoked in one click.
# completed responses, as JSON
BASE=https://app.sahelvoice.app/api/v1
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $SAHELVOICE_KEY" \
  "$BASE/surveys/suivi/responses?status=done"
import os, requests

BASE = "https://app.sahelvoice.app/api/v1"
KEY = os.environ["SAHELVOICE_KEY"]

r = requests.get(f"{BASE}/surveys/suivi/responses",
                 headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {KEY}"},
                 params={"status": "done"}, timeout=30)
for resp in r.json()["responses"]:
    print(resp["respondent"], resp["answers"]["recu"]["value"])

Frequently asked questions.

Do respondents need to install an app?

No. Everything happens in WhatsApp, which people already use. They send a keyword, scan a QR code or reply to an invitation, and the survey starts.

Do people need to be able to write?

No. Each person chooses audio, text or both, and answers with a button or a voice note. AI transcribes, your team checks.

Can we use our KoBo or ODK questionnaire?

Yes. Upload your XLSForm as it is. Anything WhatsApp cannot do, such as a photo or a GPS point, is flagged before you send.

When can we start?

Meta is completing the verification of our WhatsApp number. The first pilots start as soon as it is done. Write to us now to be part of them.

What happens to the data?

Each organisation sees only its own surveys. Phone numbers are hidden, voice notes are deleted after the period you choose, and reusing them for research needs a second, separate consent.

How much does it cost?

The first pilot is free. After that, the Organisation plan is quoted on volume. WhatsApp invitations are billed by Meta.
