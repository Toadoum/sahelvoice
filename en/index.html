Your questionnaire goes out on WhatsApp in three steps.

Your team keeps its tools. Respondents keep WhatsApp.

  1. Import your questionnaire

    Your KoBoToolbox or ODK XLSForm, as it is. SahelVoice keeps the questions, choices and conditions, and tells you what WhatsApp cannot do.

  2. Invite respondents

    Send an invitation to a contact list, or put up a keyword and a QR code. Each person agrees before the first question and can stop everything by writing STOP.

  3. Follow and check

    Answers reach your dashboard while the survey runs. Your enumerators listen to the voice notes, then you export to Excel with the audio.

What happens to a voice note.

In Chad, many people would rather speak than write. Here is the path of one voice note, from WhatsApp to your export.

  1. Transcription

    Whisper, a speech recognition model, transcribes the voice note on our servers in a few seconds.

    audio 0:04 « nous sommes sept à la maison »

  2. Reading the answer

    SahelVoice looks for the expected answer: a number said in words, a yes or a no, an option from the list, a score from 1 to 5.

    « sept » gives household_size = 7

  3. Confirmation

    When someone says a number, SahelVoice reads it back. If the answer stays unclear, it asks once more, then passes it to your enumerators.

    "You said: 7. Is that right?" Yes

  4. Human check

    Your enumerators listen to each voice note in a queue: correct, fix, or inaudible. A fix replaces the value in the export and the original stays visible.

    « cette » fixed to « sept »

We will not publish an accuracy rate until we have measured one on real Chadian answers. Your dashboard computes it from your own enumerators' fixes.

The published research behind SahelVoice.

We work on language technology for low-resource African languages, starting with Ngambay, one of the most spoken languages in southern Chad.

  • Data where there was none. The first Ngambay-French parallel corpus, open for research.
  • Models tuned for Ngambay. Three pre-trained translation models, fine-tuned on that corpus. M2M100 gives the best results.
  • The same method for voice. It guides the Ngambay and Chadian Arabic speech recognition we are preparing.

NLP4TIA 2023, Varna (Bulgaria). Proceedings in the ACL Anthology.

Respondents stay in control.

These rules live in the platform's code. No organisation can switch them off.

Consent first
SahelVoice asks for consent before the first question. Without it, we record nothing and the person appears nowhere.
STOP ends everything
The person writes STOP and gets no more messages, from any organisation, for good.
Hidden numbers
A code replaces the phone number in exports. The dashboard hides it too.
Audio deleted on time
We delete voice notes after the period the organisation chooses. Reusing them for research takes a second, separate consent.

Who builds SahelVoice.

SahelVoice is a product of Quantum Qube Technologies, a company registered in N'Djamena in 2024.

Sakayo Toadoum Sari founded the company. He was born in Chad and speaks Mbay, his mother tongue, and Ngambay, the language he has researched since 2021.

He holds a master's in industrial mathematics from AIMS Cameroon, on a Mastercard Foundation scholarship, and the African Master's in Machine Intelligence (AMMI) from AIMS Senegal, funded by Google and Meta. He also did an internship at EPFL, in Switzerland. He is now doing a PhD in artificial intelligence, on natural language processing.

His AMMI master's project produced the first Ngambay-French translation corpus and models trained on it, published in 2023 with Angela Fan and Lema Logamou Seknewna. That work now translates SahelVoice questionnaires.

He started SahelVoice so that organisations in Chad also hear the people who do not fill in forms in French.

Questions we often get.

Do respondents need to install an app?

No. Everything happens in WhatsApp, which the person already uses. They write a keyword, scan a QR code or reply to an invitation, and the survey starts.

Do people need to read and write to answer?

No. Questions arrive in writing, but the person can answer by tapping a button or sending a voice note. We transcribe the voice and your enumerators check it.

Can we use our KoBo or ODK questionnaire?

Yes. Import your XLSForm file as it is. SahelVoice keeps the questions, choices and simple conditions. Before sending, it tells you what WhatsApp cannot do, such as a photo or a GPS point.

Which languages are supported?

French today, written and spoken. For Ngambay, our own model translates questionnaires, in testing, and a speaker checks each translation. Speech recognition for Ngambay and Chadian Arabic is in preparation, with data collected only with people's consent.

What happens to the data?

Each organisation sees only its own surveys. A code replaces phone numbers in exports, we delete voice notes after the period you choose, and reusing them for research takes a second, separate consent.

How can we support SahelVoice?

Sponsor voice data collection in Chadian Arabic or Ngambay, run a survey with us, or become a research partner. Write to contact@sahelvoice.app.

How much does it cost?

The first pilots in Chad are free, and we prepare the survey with you. After that, the price depends on volume. Write to us to talk it through.
